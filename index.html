About the author

My name is Daswaget (DSWG, Daswáget). My real name is Anton Domrachev. I am a programmer, and my primary programming language is C#. I am interested in creativity and enjoy supporting other creators. In addition to software development, I also draw.

It is equally important to me that applications are intuitive, visually clean, and comfortable to use.
